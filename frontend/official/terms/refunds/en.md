## II. General Refund Principles

### 1. Digital Delivery
Many Yume Services are digital and may be delivered, activated, allocated, or consumed immediately after purchase. Once a digital benefit is delivered or substantially used, it may be non-refundable except as stated in this Policy or required by law.

### 2. Original Payment Method
Approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method. If that method is unavailable, we may use another reasonable method where permitted by law and supported by our payment providers.

### 3. Fees and Currency Conversion
Unless the refund is caused by our confirmed error or required by law, non-refundable payment platform fees, card network fees, bank fees, chargeback fees, and currency conversion differences may be deducted from the refund amount or may not be returned by third parties.

### 4. Evidence and Review
We may review order records, payment records, account status, usage logs, service delivery records, support history, and relevant evidence to determine refund eligibility.

## III. Refundable Situations
You may request a refund in the following situations:

### 1. Duplicate Payment
If the same order was charged more than once due to a system error or payment processing issue, we will refund the verified duplicate amount.

### 2. Service Not Delivered
If a paid digital Service was not delivered or activated due to an issue attributable to Yume and we cannot deliver or activate it within a reasonable time, we may provide a full or partial refund.

### 3. Major Service Failure
If a paid Service cannot function substantially as described due to a confirmed issue attributable to Yume and we cannot repair, replace, extend, or otherwise remedy the issue within a reasonable time, we may provide a full or partial refund based on the affected period, unused portion, and nature of the Service.

### 4. Unauthorized or Mistaken Charge
If you believe a charge was unauthorized or clearly mistaken, contact us promptly. We may request identity verification, payment proof, and account records. Confirmed unauthorized or erroneous charges may be refunded where appropriate.

### 5. Refund Request Window
For products, memberships, or services that are eligible for refund, you must submit the refund request within **7 days** after purchase or order submission, unless applicable law or product-specific terms provide a longer period. This Policy does not apply a separate usage-volume threshold. Refund eligibility is determined based on order status, product attributes, this Policy, applicable law, and the review method applied to the request.

### 6. Legal Requirement
If applicable law requires a refund, we will comply with that law.

## IV. Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds may be denied in the following situations:

### 1. Used or Activated Digital Benefits
- activated memberships marked as non-refundable at purchase;
- consumed, delivered, allocated, or activated digital goods, quotas, credits, storage allocations, email services, AI services, or account benefits;
- downloadable, delivered, or accessed digital content;
- custom work, deployment assistance, migration, dedicated support, or consulting after work has begun;
- special account registration privileges, identity benefits, or reserved resources that have been activated.

### 2. User-Side Issues
Refunds are generally not provided where the issue is caused by:

- incompatible device, browser, operating system, network, region, or user environment;
- failure to follow instructions, maintain backups, secure credentials, or configure third-party services correctly;
- loss caused by user deletion, user misconfiguration, or unauthorized access resulting from compromised credentials;
- dissatisfaction based only on subjective preference after substantial use.

### 3. Policy Violations or Abuse
Refunds may be denied if the account is associated with fraud, abuse, spam, unlawful use, chargeback abuse, refund manipulation, violation of the Terms, or attempts to evade refund eligibility rules.

### 4. Expired Refund Period
Refund requests submitted after the stated refund period may be denied unless required by law.

### 5. Repeat Refund Requests
For the same product, membership, or service, refund requests may not exceed **1 time**. If a refund request has already been submitted for the same product, membership, or service, we may deny any additional request unless required by law or caused by our confirmed error.

## VI. Refund Request Process

### 1. Submit Through the Order Page
Refund requests must be submitted through the self-service refund entry in the order list or order page of the website where the product, membership, or service order was submitted. If the order page does not display a refund request button, it means that you do not meet the refund request conditions shown by the system.

### 2. Automatic or Manual Review
After a refund request is successfully submitted, the system will determine whether the request is subject to automatic review or manual review. If automatic review applies, the refund will be automatically approved after successful submission, and the arrival time will follow the processing time stated in this Policy. If manual review applies, we will complete the manual review within **5 business days** after receiving sufficient information.

### 3. Manual Support Request
If the order page does not display a refund request button but you believe you still meet the refund request conditions, you may contact customer support by email at **support@yume.zone** to request manual application. Please include your order number or transaction ID, account email or user identifier, product name, purchase date, refund reason, and any relevant screenshots, logs, receipts, or support ticket information.

### 4. Processing Time
Approved refunds are submitted to the payment provider and generally arrive within **3 to 15 business days**, depending on your bank, card network, wallet, payment provider, currency, and region.
